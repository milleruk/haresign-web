Who we are

This website is operated by Haresign Consulting Services, a sole trader business providing practice and business management consulting for GP practices and Primary Care Networks across England.

The data controller is Ben Haresign. You can contact us at {{ legal.contact_email }}.

What this notice covers

This notice covers the public Haresign website only — the pages you are reading now, including Insights articles.

Haresign Intelligence, Haresign Community, Haresign Workspace and Haresign Account are separate services. Where they process personal data they do so under their own arrangements, and using them may involve additional or service-specific privacy information. Nothing in this notice describes them.

What information we collect

This website is deliberately minimal in what it collects.

Information you send us

There are no forms on this website. Where the site invites you to get in touch, it opens an email to us. If you email us we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to write.

Technical information

Like any website, our servers keep access logs. These record the request made, the time, the response, your IP address, and the browser user-agent string. They exist to keep the service running securely and to diagnose faults.

Analytics

This website does not use analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, and no third-party measurement of any kind. The site makes no requests to any external service: fonts, styles, scripts and images are all served from our own servers.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies when you browse it. See the Cookie Policy for detail, including the cookies used by the staff area.

How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Email enquiries — used only to reply to you and to carry on any resulting conversation. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, in responding to an enquiry you have initiated.
  • Server logs — used to operate the site securely and investigate problems. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, in keeping the service available and secure.

We do not use your information for marketing without your explicit agreement, and we do not add you to any mailing list because you emailed us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

We use suppliers who process information on our behalf in order to run the service — a hosting provider, and an email provider for correspondence. They act on our instructions and are not permitted to use the information for anything else.

How long we keep it

  • Email correspondence — kept for as long as it remains relevant to the enquiry or the working relationship, then deleted.
  • Server logs — kept only as long as needed to operate and secure the service, then rotated and discarded.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the systems behind it is restricted to those who need it, and the staff publishing area requires an account.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate;
  • ask us to delete information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, our processing;
  • ask for a copy of information you provided, in a portable form;
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise any of these, email {{ legal.contact_email }}.

Other Haresign services

Links from this site to Haresign Consulting, Haresign Intelligence, Haresign Community, Haresign Workspace or Haresign Account take you to separate services. Some are not yet available. When you use one, its own privacy information applies in addition to this notice, and this notice does not describe what it collects.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The “last reviewed” date at the top of the page reflects the most recent change.

Contact and complaints

Email {{ legal.contact_email }} with any question about this notice or about how we handle your information.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would ask you to raise it with us first so we have the chance to put it right.
