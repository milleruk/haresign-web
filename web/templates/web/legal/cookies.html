What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to store. They are used to make sites work, to keep them secure, and — on many sites — to measure how they are used. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Notice.

What this site uses

This website sets one cookie, and only where you could sign up to the newsletter. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party content — so there is nothing to consent to, and no cookie banner.

That is a description of how the site is built today, not a promise about forever. If it changes, this page changes with it, and anything non-essential will ask for your consent before it runs.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary for a site to work securely. They do not require consent, and they cannot be switched off without breaking the thing they protect.

Pages carrying the newsletter sign-up — the homepage, Insights and articles — set a security token so that the form cannot be submitted from another site on your behalf. Pages without the form, such as this one, set nothing at all.

Every cookie this website sets.
NamePurposeWhen it is setHow long it lasts
csrftoken Protects the newsletter form against cross-site request forgery — it stops another website submitting the form as you. On any page showing the newsletter sign-up. One year.
sessionid Keeps a signed-in member of staff signed in. Only on signing in to the staff publishing area at /admin/, which is not part of the public site. Until sign-out.

Analytics and optional cookies

None are currently used. This site performs no measurement of its visitors and sets no optional cookies.

If analytics is introduced later it will be listed here in the same form as the table above, it will be optional, and it will not run until you have agreed to it.

Third-party content

This site loads nothing from anyone else. Fonts, stylesheets, scripts and images are all served from our own servers, so no third party sees your visit or can set a cookie through it.

Links to other Haresign services, or to external sites such as NHS England, take you somewhere we do not control. Those sites have their own cookie policies.

Controlling cookies

Because this site sets no optional cookies, there is nothing here to opt out of. Nothing you do or do not accept changes what we learn about you, because we are not measuring it.

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies through its settings, and you can apply that to this site as to any other. Blocking cookies will not affect your reading of any page here; it would stop the newsletter form and the staff publishing area from working, since both depend on the security token above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “last reviewed” date at the top of the page reflects the most recent change.
